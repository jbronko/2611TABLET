How to play

Dig with your finger

Drag across the sand to carve a channel. The sand piles up along its edges. Use more fingers to dig faster.

The tide comes in

It starts rising the moment you dig. Waves wash up the beach and fill anything that lies low enough.

Water shapes the sand

Fast water carves, slow water drops its sand, and thin walls collapse. Freshly dug sand holds a steep edge until it dries.

Keep the water in

Stars come from how full the pools are once the tide goes back out. Use Build to pile sand into a pool’s gap after it fills.

Check your depth

Turn on Depth to tint the sand the high tide can reach. If your channel is tinted all the way from the sea to the pool, the water will get there.
