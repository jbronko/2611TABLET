A sand and water game

Tide Digger

Dig channels with your finger to lead the incoming tide into the rock pools. When the tide goes back out, the pools need to stay full.

How to play

  • Drag a finger across the sand to dig. Sand piles up along the edges of your trench. Use more fingers to dig faster.
  • The tide starts coming in when you first dig. Waves wash up the beach and fill anything that's low enough.
  • Water carves its own path: fast water washes sand away, slow water drops it, and thin walls collapse.
  • Switch to Build to pile sand into dams, and Smooth to flatten it.
  • Stars come from how full the pools still are once the tide has gone out. Dam the gap to keep more water in.
